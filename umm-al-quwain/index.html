Al Taher Group fabricates and installs metalwork, aluminium, glass and joinery for Umm Al Quwain from our workshop in the Al Hail Industrial Area, Fujairah. Villa gates, stair railings, aluminium and uPVC windows, glass works, fitted kitchens, pergolas and steel fabrication: all made in-house and installed by our own team. Free site visit within 48 hours; itemised quotation within 24 hours.

How we work

  1. Free site visit within 48 hours: we measure the actual structure, not a drawing.
  2. Itemised quotation within 24 hours of the visit: priced line by line.
  3. Fabrication in our own Fujairah workshop: no subcontracting.
  4. Installation by our own team, with committed dates in the quotation.

What work do we do in Umm Al Quwain?

Predominantly villa and residential: entrance gates with matching boundary railings, internal staircases and balustrades, window and door packages, glass partitions and shower enclosures, fitted kitchens and outdoor shade structures. UAQ's coastal position means the same specification discipline we apply in Fujairah: properly prepared powder coating and corrosion-resistant fittings on anything exposed to salt air.

Which services do we deliver here?

  • Custom metal gates: cast aluminium, steel and automated systems.
  • Stair railings and balustrades: stainless steel, glass, aluminium and iron.
  • Aluminium windows and doors: thermal break profiles and Low-E glazing.
  • Glass works: partitions, shower enclosures and balustrades.
  • Metal and steel fabrication: structural steel, sheet metal, bollards and fencing.
  • Curtain walls and facade systems: stick and unitised glazing.

How does the delivery radius affect a UAQ project?

Practically, it means we plan around fewer, longer site visits rather than frequent short ones. Measurement is done thoroughly up front so fabrication is correct first time; installation is mobilised in a defined window. Fabrication lead times are unaffected: a gate is still roughly 2 to 3 weeks from approval, railings 1 to 2 weeks, a kitchen 3 to 5 weeks. What matters is that the dates are committed in writing before work begins rather than estimated loosely.

Why choose an out-of-emirate workshop?

The trade is control against proximity. Everything we quote is fabricated, finished and installed by us: no subcontracted powder coating, no third-party glazier, no chain of margins. For a villa package where finishes need to match across gate, railings and joinery, running it all through one workshop and one coating batch gives a result that separate local suppliers struggle to match. For urgent small repairs, local is better, and we will tell you honestly which situation you are in.

Frequently asked questions

Does Al Taher work in Umm Al Quwain?

Yes. We fabricate in our Fujairah workshop and install across UAQ with our own team: gates, railings, windows, glass, kitchens, pergolas and metal fabrication.

How long do projects take in UAQ?

Fabrication times are the same as anywhere: gates roughly 2 to 3 weeks from design approval, railings 1 to 2 weeks, kitchens 3 to 5 weeks. Distance affects site-visit scheduling rather than production time.

Do you charge extra for the distance?

Mobilisation and delivery appear as an itemised line in the quotation rather than being absorbed into rates, so it is visible and you can weigh it.

Does coastal air in UAQ affect the specification?

Yes, the same way it does in Fujairah. We specify properly prepared powder coating and corrosion-resistant fittings for exposed gates, railings and balustrades, because coatings and hardware fail before the base metal does.

Can you handle a full villa package?

Yes. Gates, railings, windows, glass, kitchens and joinery can run as one package under a single project manager, with finishes matched across trades through our own powder coating line.
