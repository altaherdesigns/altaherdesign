Steel fabrication in Sharjah

Structural steel, gates, railings and architectural metalwork for Sharjah: fabricated in our own workshop, coated on our own line, installed by our own crew.

Home › Areas › Steel Fabrication Sharjah

If you are comparing steel fabrication companies in Sharjah, the first filter is simple: who actually fabricates, and who coordinates other people's workshops? Al Taher Group has run its own fabrication workshop since 1984: structural steel, sheet metal, gates, railings and architectural metalwork, cut, welded, finished and powder-coated in one facility, then installed in Sharjah by our own crew. Site visits in Sharjah within 48 hours; itemised quotation within 24 hours of the visit.

What steel fabrication work do we deliver in Sharjah?

Sharjah's mix skews industrial and commercial more than any other emirate we serve: mezzanine floors and platforms for the industrial areas, warehouse structural steel, sheet-metal enclosures and machine guards, safety bollards for loading bays, boundary fencing and heavy-duty gates. On the residential side (Al Nahda, Muwaileh, Al Suyoh and the villa communities), the demand is villa gates, stair railings and car shades. Both run through the same workshop and the same powder-coating line.

Why choose a Fujairah workshop for a Sharjah project?

Because the workshop matters more than the postcode. Fabrication happens off-site by definition: what your project experiences is the survey, the delivery and the installation. We survey Sharjah sites on scheduled visits, fabricate to confirmed dimensions, and install in planned windows with our own team. What you gain from our setup is control: no subcontracted welding, no third-party coating queue, one accountable party from measurement to handover. Any transport line appears itemised in the quotation, not hidden in the rates.

How do we work with Sharjah contractors?

We price from tender drawings, work to the main contractor's programme, conduct our own surveys and install with our own crew. For fit-out firms, we cover the metal, aluminium and glass scope that is rarely produced in-house. The same 48-hour visit and 24-hour quotation commitment applies to contractor enquiries.

What does steel fabrication cost in Sharjah?

Structural work is priced from drawings by weight and complexity; architectural items (gates, railings, fencing) are priced per piece after a site measure. Every quotation is itemised line by line: materials, fabrication, finish, delivery and installation, so alternatives can be compared on substance rather than a single lump sum.

Frequently asked questions

Do you install in Sharjah's industrial areas?

Yes. Industrial-area work is a large share of our Sharjah activity: mezzanines, platforms, sheet-metal enclosures, bollards and heavy gates, surveyed on site and installed by our own crew.

How fast can you visit a Sharjah site?

Within 48 hours of your enquiry, anywhere in the emirate, and the itemised quotation follows within 24 hours of that visit.

Do you handle both structural and architectural steel?

Yes, structural packages from drawings, and architectural metalwork such as gates, railings and fencing, all fabricated in the same workshop and finished on our own powder-coating line.

Can you match an existing gate or railing in Sharjah?

Usually, yes. We measure the existing piece, match sections and ornament where feasible, and coat in the same RAL colour so additions read as one installation.

Do you work as a subcontractor to Sharjah main contractors?

Yes. We price from tender drawings, work to the programme, and carry our scope from survey through installation with one accountable team.
