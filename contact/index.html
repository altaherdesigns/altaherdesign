Yes: we serve all 7 UAE emirates. Our workshop is based in Al Hail Industrial Area, Fujairah, but we deliver and install across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ras Al Khaimah, Ajman and Umm Al Quwain.

The cost depends on size, material (mild steel, stainless or aluminium), design complexity and powder-coating finish. We provide free, detailed written quotes. Send your dimensions or a photo via WhatsApp for a fast estimate.

Powder coating is a dry finishing process where electrostatically charged powder is applied to metal and cured under heat. It produces a durable, weather-resistant finish that outperforms wet paint: especially important in the UAE's coastal heat, salt air and dust. Our DAT division runs its own in-house coating line with all RAL colours available.

Most standard pergola installations take 3 to 7 working days from delivery to completion. A firm timeline is provided with every written quote after the site survey.
