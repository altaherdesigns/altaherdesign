What glass work do we cover?

Framed or frameless: what actually differs?

Frameless is not simply "better": it is a different set of trade-offs. Framed systems hold glass in aluminium profiles, which means better acoustic sealing, easier door alignment and lower cost. Frameless removes the profile, so the glass itself must do structural work: typically 10 to 12mm toughened rather than 6 to 8mm, with patch fittings or channels engineered for the load. You gain a cleaner look and lose some acoustic performance and budget headroom. For a meeting room where sound privacy matters, framed with acoustic seals usually outperforms frameless.

Why is safety glass non-negotiable?

Shower enclosures and balustrades are safety-critical, and both require toughened (tempered) glass, which fragments into blunt granules instead of shards, or toughened-laminated, which additionally holds together on an interlayer when broken. Ordinary annealed glass is not acceptable in either application. Balustrade glass also has to be fixed in hardware rated for the load, and on a Fujairah balcony the fittings, not the glass, are what corrode first. We specify glass and fittings together for that reason. Full glass specification guide

Why do we template after tiling?

Most leaking shower enclosures are measuring failures, not glass failures. Walls are rarely plumb and tiled corners rarely square, so an enclosure cut to the architect's dimensions will not seal against the room as built. We template after tiling is complete, cut and edge to the real opening, and drill hinge positions to suit. It adds a visit; it removes the leak.

As a package, on a programme, with the paperwork contractors expect. Frequently asked questions

What glass is required for a shower enclosure?

Toughened (tempered) safety glass, typically 8 to 10mm for framed enclosures and 10 to 12mm for frameless. It fragments into blunt granules rather than shards if broken. Ordinary annealed glass is not acceptable in a shower.

Can you make a frameless glass partition for an office?

Yes: frameless partitions in 10 to 12mm toughened glass with minimal patch fittings, plus framed aluminium systems where acoustic sealing or door alignment matters more than the seamless look. We advise which suits the room during the site visit.

How long does glass work take?

Most partitions, enclosures and balustrades are fabricated and installed within 1 to 2 weeks of templating, depending on glass specification and hardware. Templating happens after tiling or structural work is finished, and committed dates go into the quotation.

Do glass balustrades survive coastal air in Fujairah?

The glass is unaffected by salt air; the fittings are the vulnerable part. We use corrosion-resistant stainless or powder-coated aluminium channels and clamps on anything exposed, which is what determines whether an outdoor balustrade still looks right in five years.

Do you replace glass in existing frames?

Yes. We re-glaze existing aluminium frames and replace broken toughened panels in showers, balustrades and shopfronts, as well as supplying complete new systems.

Can you fit a shower screen in an existing bathroom?

Yes. We template the screen after the tiles are on, to the walls and floor as they are, so it seals even where they are not perfectly straight.

Can you make arched glass doors and windows?

Yes. Arched openings are measured on site once the arch is finished, and the frame and the glass are made to that template.

Can a sliding glass door go into an existing opening?

Usually. It depends on the width, the support above the opening and the floor level, which we check at the free site visit before we quote.
