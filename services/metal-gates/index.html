Which gate material should you choose?

MaterialStrengthsBest suited to
Cast aluminiumNever rusts, holds intricate ornamental detail, lighter on motorsCoastal villas, decorative and classical designs
SteelHighest strength per section, best for wide spans and securityLarge driveways, laser-cut panel designs, commercial
Wrought ironFully bespoke traditional patterns, substantial feelOrnate and heritage-style entrances
Aluminium slatClean modern lines, lightweight, privacy infill optionsContemporary minimalist elevations

The coast usually decides it. Cast aluminium removes rust risk entirely, which matters within reach of salt air. Steel is stronger and takes modern laser-cut panels well — and with correct preparation and powder coating it performs perfectly well here too. We fabricate all four and will tell you which your span, design and budget actually call for.

Sliding or swing?

Space decides this more than taste. Swing gates need clear arc room inside the boundary and suit driveways with depth; they are the classic villa look and simpler mechanically. Sliding gates run along the boundary line and need no swing space, which makes them the answer for short driveways or where the ground slopes. Cantilever sliding systems avoid a floor track altogether — useful where sand and debris would foul a track. We survey the opening and levels before recommending either.

Is gate automation worth it?

Motors, remotes, keypads, intercom integration and safety sensors — specified and installed with the gate rather than bolted on afterwards, which is what keeps the mechanism aligned to the leaf weight. Automation is sized to the gate: an underpowered motor on a heavy cast gate is the single most common cause of premature failure. Automatic gates explained →

Why does the finish decide the lifespan?

A gate is fully exposed, every day, often near the sea. The metal rarely fails first — the coating does. We powder-coat on our own line at proper preparation and film-thickness standards, because outsourced finishing is where gate quality is usually lost. Our powder coating → · Powder vs paint →

Can railings, fencing and side doors match the gate?

Most gate projects extend to the boundary. We fabricate matching pedestrian side doors, boundary railings and metal fencing in the same pattern and the same powder-coat batch, so the whole entrance reads as one piece of work rather than three approximations.

What does gate maintenance involve?

Very little, if the gate was made properly — which is rather the point. A powder-coated gate needs an occasional wash to remove salt and dust, a yearly check of hinges or rollers with light lubrication, and, on automated gates, a periodic test of the safety sensors and remote batteries. What it should not need is repainting every two years — that schedule is the signature of a wet-painted or poorly prepared gate. We also service and refurbish existing gates, including re-coating older gates on our line where the base metal is sound, which is often far cheaper than replacement.

What makes a good villa gate?

A villa gate earns its place on three counts: presence, security and survival in the climate. Presence comes from proportion and ornament matched to the facade — a gate too short for a tall frontage weakens the whole elevation. Security comes from the leaf construction, the locking points and, increasingly, automation with safety sensors. Survival is the finish: on a coastal villa the powder coating decides whether the gate still looks new in year five. The strongest villa results pair the gate with matching boundary fencing and a side pedestrian door, all coated in one batch.

When is a sliding gate the right choice?

When the driveway is short, the plot slopes, or the entrance is wide. A sliding gate runs along the boundary wall on a track — or cantilevers clear of the ground entirely where sand would foul a floor track — so it needs no swing space in front. It suits wide entrances that would demand very heavy swing leaves, and it automates particularly well: one motor, one leaf, predictable travel. The trade is side room: the wall must have clear run-off length to receive the open gate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a custom gate cost in the UAE?

It depends on span, material, pattern complexity, finish and whether automation is included, so we quote from an actual site measurement rather than a price list. The quotation is itemised and arrives within 24 hours of the visit.

How long does a custom gate take?

Typically 2–3 weeks from design approval to installation, depending on size, pattern and finish. Automation adds a little. Because fabrication and powder coating both happen in our workshop, your gate is not queued behind a third-party finisher.

Cast aluminium or steel for a UAE villa gate?

Cast aluminium if rust resistance and ornamental detail matter most — especially near the coast. Steel if you need very wide spans, maximum security or modern laser-cut panels. Both perform well when properly coated.

Can you automate an existing gate?

Often yes. During the site visit we assess the gate's weight, frame condition and hinge or track arrangement, then advise whether automation can be retrofitted safely or whether the structure needs strengthening first.

Do you make matching fencing and side doors?

Yes — matching is the usual request. The same pattern, profile and powder-coat colour run through the main gate, pedestrian door and boundary railings.

How much does a villa gate cost in the UAE?

It depends on width, material, ornament and automation — which is why we price after a real site measure rather than from a list. The itemised quotation arrives within 24 hours of the visit, with gate, coating, automation and installation each as its own line.

Sliding gate or swing gate for a wide entrance?

Wide entrances usually favour sliding: one leaf, no swing space consumed, easier automation. Cantilever sliding systems remove the floor track entirely where wind-blown sand would foul it. Swing remains right where the boundary has no side run-off room.
