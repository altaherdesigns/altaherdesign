Carpentry is where Al Taher began in 1984, and the joinery workshop still runs beside our metal and glass shops in the Al Hail Industrial Area, Fujairah. We make wooden doors, wardrobes, TV walls, kitchens, wall panelling and built in furniture for villas, apartments, offices and shops across the UAE: measured on your actual walls, built in our own workshop and installed by our own team, with no subcontracted steps. When a design mixes materials (a kitchen island on a steel frame, a marble worktop, glass fronted cabinets), every trade is under one roof.

Which wood, board or composite goes where?

The room decides the material, not the catalogue. Heat and humidity are what age joinery in the UAE: a board that is fine in a dry bedroom can swell under a sink or against a damp outside wall within a few summers. This is what we usually suggest, room by room:

What makes joinery last in UAE humidity?

Three things decide it: the board, the edges and the hardware. BWP plywood resists humidity far better than MDF and holds screws and hinges for years, so it is our usual choice where water is near. MDF and particle board are fine in dry rooms when every edge is sealed, because an open edge is where moisture gets in. Hinges and drawer runners decide whether doors still close softly after thousands of openings, and cheap hardware is usually the first thing to fail. We specify all three to the room's exposure before we talk about colours, and we tell you where spending more genuinely pays back.

How much does carpentry work cost in the UAE?

Carpentry is priced item by item, from the sizes we measure. Kitchens start from AED 1,300 per running metre. Doors, wardrobes, TV walls and furniture are priced by size, material, finish and hardware, so a figure before measuring would be a guess. The itemised quotation arrives within 24 hours of the free site visit, with each item, each finish and the installation on its own line, so you can change one choice and see what it does to the total.

  • Material: laminate costs least; veneer, lacquer and solid wood cost more
  • Size and height: floor to ceiling units take more board and more hardware
  • Hardware: soft close hinges and full extension drawers cost more and last longer
  • Carving and detail: carved doors and arched panels take more workshop time

For kitchen budgets, read what a fitted kitchen costs in the UAE.

How long does carpentry work take?

Most joinery is made in the workshop while your site gets ready. A kitchen typically takes 3 to 5 weeks from design approval, then 2 to 4 days to install, and lacquered or solid wood finishes take longer than laminate and acrylic. Wardrobes, doors and TV walls follow the same order: measure, approve, build, install, with committed dates written into the quotation. In new homes we take the final measurement once tiling and MEP work are done, because joinery built to a drawing rather than to the finished room is where fitted furniture goes wrong.

Frequently asked questions

What carpentry work do you do?

Wooden doors, wardrobes and dressing rooms, kitchens, TV walls, wall panelling, built in furniture and commercial joinery such as reception counters and timber screens. Everything is measured on site, made in our Fujairah workshop and installed by our own team.

Do you make solid wood doors?

Yes. Solid wood main and interior doors come from our own carpentry workshop, carved, panelled, flush or sliding, made to the opening we measure. For bathrooms and kitchens we usually suggest WPC, which does not swell.

Which material lasts best in UAE humidity?

Where water is near, BWP plywood or aluminium carcasses and WPC doors. In dry rooms, board with properly sealed edges, veneer and solid wood all last well. We choose by the room's exposure, not by the catalogue.

How much does carpentry work cost?

It is priced item by item from measured sizes. Kitchens start from AED 1,300 per running metre; doors, wardrobes and TV walls depend on size, material, finish and hardware. The itemised quotation follows within 24 hours of the free site visit.

Is your carpentry workshop in Fujairah?

Yes. The joinery workshop is in the Al Hail Industrial Area, Fujairah, beside our metal, aluminium and glass shops. You are welcome to visit and see materials and finishes before you decide.

Do you send a carpenter to Dibba, Masafi, Kalba and Khorfakkan?

Yes. We work across Fujairah, from Fujairah City to Dibba Al Fujairah, Masafi, Mirbah and Qidfa, in the east coast towns of Kalba and Khorfakkan, and in Hatta. The site visit is free and takes place within 48 hours.

Do you do carpentry work in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah?

Yes. We measure, build and install across all seven emirates. The joinery is made in our Fujairah workshop and fitted by the same team that measured it.

Can you match new joinery to doors or furniture I already have?

Usually, yes. Send photos on WhatsApp and we tell you how close we can get in wood, veneer or a painted finish, and what it would cost.

Do you refinish old wooden doors?

Yes, where the timber is sound. A worn wooden door can be sanded and refinished instead of replaced; if the door has warped or the frame has failed, we say so and quote a new one.

Can you build from my architect's or interior designer's drawings?

Yes. We build to the approved drawings, and we still measure the room as built before cutting, so doors, wardrobes and panelling fit the walls you actually have.
