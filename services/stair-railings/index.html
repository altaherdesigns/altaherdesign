Handrail or balustrade — what's the difference?

The balustrade is the whole barrier: posts, infill and top rail. The handrail is the part you grip. They need designing together, because grip height, infill spacing and post positions interact — and a handrail retrofitted to an existing balustrade rarely sits at a comfortable height.

Why we template on site

Staircases are almost never built exactly as drawn. Rises vary by millimetres, landings are rarely perfectly square, and a railing fabricated from the architect's drawing ends up with uneven gaps or posts that miss their fixings. We template the actual stair once treads are in, fabricate to those measurements, and install with fixings the structure will genuinely take.

Indoor versus outdoor

Indoor railings are chosen mainly on appearance. Outdoors — balconies, terraces, roof edges — salt air changes the specification: stainless or properly powder-coated aluminium, corrosion-resistant fittings, and toughened or laminated glass where glass is used. On coastal balconies it is nearly always the fittings that fail first, not the material, which is why we specify both together.

Safety glass in balustrades

Glass balustrades use toughened or toughened-laminated glass — never annealed — fixed in channels, clamps or standoff pins rated for the load. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a handrail and a balustrade?

The balustrade is the entire barrier — posts, infill and rail. The handrail is the graspable part. They should be designed together, since grip height and infill spacing depend on each other.

Are glass balustrades safe on staircases?

Yes when specified correctly: toughened or toughened-laminated glass fixed in load-rated channels, clamps or standoff pins. Ordinary annealed glass is never acceptable. The fittings carry the safety function as much as the glass does.

Which railing material lasts longest outdoors in the UAE?

Stainless steel and properly powder-coated aluminium both perform well. The deciding factor outdoors is usually fitting quality — cheap fixings corrode on a coastal balcony long before the railing material itself does.

How long does a stair railing take?

Typically 1–2 weeks from design approval. Site templating happens first so the railing is fabricated to your actual staircase rather than to a drawing.

Can you match a new railing to existing metalwork?

Yes. Matching an existing gate pattern or a balustrade elsewhere in the property is routine — the same profiles and powder-coat colour run through both.
