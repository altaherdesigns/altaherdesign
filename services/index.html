Straight answers on price and timelines

How do Al Taher's prices compare to smaller workshops?

Often closer than people expect, and sometimes lower, because nothing is subcontracted. A small workshop that outsources powder coating, glass or welding pays a margin at each step and passes it on. We fabricate, coat and install with our own team, so there is no markup chain. You pay for materials, our labour and one margin, not three. For a like-for-like specification, ask us to quote against any other offer and compare line by line.

How long does a residential project take? Do homeowners wait behind big contracts?

No, residential and commercial work run on separate schedules, and every job gets a committed timeline in the quotation before we start. Typical guide times once design is approved: a custom gate runs about 2 to 3 weeks, stair railings 1 to 2 weeks, and a full kitchen 3 to 5 weeks, depending on finish and season. Because fabrication happens in our own workshop, we control the queue: your job is never waiting on a third-party fabricator.

Do I have to deal with two different companies for a mixed project?

No. Al Taher operates two licensed divisions (carpentry/aluminium and metal fabrication), but you get one project manager who owns your job across both. One quotation, one schedule, one point of contact from site visit to handover.
