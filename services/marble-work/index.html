Marble Work
Floors, Staircases, Countertops & Cladding

Marble supplied, templated and installed for UAE villas — with the metal fabrication and joinery trades marble usually meets sitting in the same workshop.

Home › Services › Marble Work

Al Taher supplies and installs marble flooring, staircases, countertops and wall cladding across the UAE from our Fujairah workshop. What separates us from a stone-only supplier is what sits next door: metal fabrication, glass and joinery under the same roof. On the projects where marble meets another trade — most obviously a marble staircase with a metal or glass balustrade — that removes the coordination gap where tolerances usually go wrong.

How we work

  1. Free site visit within 48 hours — we measure the actual structure, not a drawing.
  2. Itemised quotation within 24 hours of the visit — materials, finish, hardware and installation priced line by line.
  3. Fabrication in our own Fujairah workshop — no subcontracting.
  4. Installation by our own team, with committed dates written into the quotation.

What marble work do we deliver?

  • Flooring — entrances, living areas, corridors and bathrooms, laid to the room rather than to a drawing.
  • Staircases — marble treads, risers and nosings, including marble-and-metal designs.
  • Countertops and vanities — templated after cabinets are set, with sink and hob cut-outs machined in the workshop.
  • Wall cladding and feature panels — bookmatched slabs, reception walls, fireplace surrounds.
  • Skirting, thresholds and window sills — the detail work that makes stone installations look finished.

How do you choose the right stone?

Hardness and finish are engineering decisions; colour and veining are taste. High-traffic floors want denser stones that keep their polish under grit and foot traffic. Bathrooms favour honed finishes for grip. Outdoors, polished marble becomes slippery when wet and dulls in direct sun, so flamed or honed is the correct call. We put physical samples in your actual light and layout before a slab is cut, because stone rarely looks the same in a showroom as it does in your entrance hall.

Marble-and-metal staircases: why one workshop matters

The signature modern UAE staircase — marble treads on a steel stringer with glass or stainless balustrades — is two trades pretending to be one. The stone must be templated to the metal, balustrade fixings must land where the stone permits, and both sets of tolerances have to agree. When the marble team and the metal fabrication team share a workshop, that conversation happens at the bench instead of between two subcontractors' schedules. Full marble guide →

What drives marble cost?

Stone choice, more than anything else — the spread between a dependable beige marble and an imported statement stone is several-fold per square metre before any labour. After that: surface finish, edge profiles, stair nosing details and installation complexity. We quote per area, itemised, so the budget conversation happens against real numbers rather than a lump sum.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which marble is best for UAE villa flooring?

Harder, denser stones hold their polish better under traffic and grit, and lighter tones stay cooler underfoot — which suits the climate. Softer decorative stones are better kept to feature walls and low-traffic areas. We match stone to how each room is actually used.

Can you do a marble staircase with a metal or glass balustrade?

Yes — and it is one of the projects we are best placed for, because the marble, the metal fabrication and the glass all happen in our own workshop. The stone and the balustrade are templated to each other rather than coordinated between separate subcontractors.

Is marble suitable outdoors in the UAE?

With the right stone and finish, yes. Outdoors we specify flamed or honed finishes for grip and denser stones that tolerate heat and salt air. High-polish marble outdoors becomes slippery when wet and loses its sheen faster in direct sun.

When should countertops be templated?

After the cabinets are installed, never before. We template the actual carcass dimensions, then cut and edge the stone with sink and hob openings machined in the workshop rather than improvised on site.
