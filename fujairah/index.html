Why does a local workshop matter in Fujairah?

Three reasons, all practical. Site visits happen quickly because we are minutes from most of the emirate rather than hours away. Remedial work — an adjustment, a re-coat, an aftersales call — is a short drive, not a scheduling problem. And because fabrication, coating and installation sit under one roof here, one team is accountable from measurement to handover instead of a chain of subcontractors.

What does the coastal climate change?

Salt-laden air off the Gulf of Oman attacks poorly finished metal — blistering, chalking and peeling are the usual failures, and they appear within a couple of seasons rather than a decade. Finishing matters more here than inland, which is why we run our own powder coating line rather than sending frames to a third party. Why powder coating beats paint →

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Al Taher's workshop in Fujairah?

Our workshop is in the Al Hail Industrial Area, Fujairah — the same site the business has operated from since 1984. Metal fabrication, aluminium, glass, joinery and powder coating all happen there, which is how we control quality and lead times instead of waiting on a third-party fabricator.

Do you cover the whole emirate, including the East Coast?

Yes. We have completed work in Fujairah City, Mohammed Bin Zayed City, Kalba, Farfar, Al Hail, Al Faseel, Sakamkam, Dibba, Khor Fakkan, Masafi, Qidfa, Mirbah, Al Bidiyah and Merashid. Being based on the East Coast means a site visit in Kalba or Khor Fakkan is a short drive, not a cross-emirate journey.

How quickly can you visit and quote?

We offer a free site visit within 48 hours anywhere in Fujairah, and send an itemised quotation within 24 hours of that visit. No charge for either, and no obligation to proceed.

What size of project do you take on?

Everything from a single stair railing or replacement door to complete villa and building packages. Across Fujairah and Sharjah we have completed 600+ projects since 1984.

How long does the work take?

Once design is approved: a custom gate is typically 2–3 weeks, stair railings 1–2 weeks, a fitted kitchen 3–5 weeks, depending on finish and season. Because fabrication happens in our own workshop, your job is never queued behind a third-party fabricator.
