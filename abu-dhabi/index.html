Al Taher Group supplies, fabricates and installs metalwork, aluminium, glass and facade systems for Abu Dhabi projects from our workshop in the Al Hail Industrial Area, Fujairah. The work splits between trade packages for contractors and fit-out firms, and direct villa work — gates, railings, windows, kitchens and pergolas. Free site visit within 48 hours; itemised quotation within 24 hours.

How we work

  1. Free site visit within 48 hours — we measure the actual structure, not a drawing.
  2. Itemised quotation within 24 hours of the visit — priced line by line.
  3. Fabrication in our own Fujairah workshop — no subcontracting.
  4. Installation by our own team, with committed dates in the quotation.

What does Abu Dhabi work usually involve?

In our experience the emirate divides into two very different jobs. Commercial and institutional work tends to arrive as a defined package against drawings — structural steel, balustrades, cladding sub-frames, shopfronts or glazing — with a main contractor programme to hit and documentation to match. Villa work in the residential districts is design-led and iterative: gates, staircases, glass and joinery specified together, where matching finishes across trades matters more than raw speed. We handle both, but they are quoted and scheduled quite differently.

Which services do we deliver here?

  • Custom metal gates — cast aluminium, steel and automated systems.
  • Stair railings and balustrades — stainless steel, glass, aluminium and iron.
  • Aluminium windows and doors — thermal break profiles and Low-E glazing.
  • Glass works — partitions, shower enclosures and balustrades.
  • Metal and steel fabrication — structural steel, sheet metal, bollards and fencing.
  • Curtain walls and facade systems — stick and unitised glazing.

How does distance affect an Abu Dhabi project?

It affects mobilisation, not workmanship — and it is worth being straight about that. Abu Dhabi is the furthest emirate from our workshop, so we plan installation in consolidated periods rather than daily attendance, and we front-load site measurement so fabrication is right first time. For a large package with a defined programme, that works well and clients rarely notice the distance. For work needing frequent short visits or immediate callout, an Abu Dhabi workshop is the more sensible choice, and we will say so rather than take a job we cannot service properly.

Coordination across trades

Al Taher runs carpentry, aluminium, glass and metal fabrication from one premises under two licensed divisions. On a project mixing a marble staircase with a metal balustrade, or a fitted kitchen with a steel-framed island, that means one project manager and one schedule rather than three subcontractors negotiating tolerances. Frequently Asked Questions

Does Al Taher take on projects in Abu Dhabi?

Yes — both trade packages for contractors and fit-out firms, and direct residential work. Fabrication happens in our Fujairah workshop and installation is by our own team.

How do you manage installation at that distance?

We consolidate installation into defined mobilisation periods rather than daily attendance, and we front-load site measurement so fabrication is correct first time. Programme dates are committed in the quotation.

Do you quote from tender drawings?

Yes. We price from drawings for contractor packages and confirm dimensions with our own site survey before fabrication begins.

Is Abu Dhabi work more expensive?

Mobilisation appears as an itemised line rather than being built into rates, so you can see it. On package-scale work it is a modest proportion; on very small jobs it can make a local workshop the better choice, and we will tell you if so.

Which services are most commonly requested in Abu Dhabi?

Structural steel and fabrication, balustrades, curtain walling and cladding on the commercial side; gates, railings, kitchens and glass on villa projects.
