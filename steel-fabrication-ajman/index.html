Steel fabrication in Ajman

Structural steel, gates, railings and architectural metalwork for Ajman: fabricated in our own workshop, coated on our own line, installed by our own crew.

Home › Areas › Steel Fabrication Ajman

Looking for a steel fabrication company in Ajman? The practical question is whether your fabricator controls its own workshop, its own finishing and its own installers. Al Taher Group does all three: fabricating since 1984 and installing across Ajman with our own crew, from Al Jurf's industrial plots to the villa communities of Al Mowaihat, Al Rawda and Al Zahya. Site visit within 48 hours; itemised quotation within 24 hours of the visit.

What do Ajman clients ask us to build?

Ajman's demand is villa-led: entrance gates with matching boundary fencing, stair railings and balcony balustrades, car shades and pergolas, and window security grilles. On the commercial side we fabricate structural steel for workshops and showrooms, storage mezzanines, and safety bollards for forecourts. Everything is coated on our own powder-coating line before delivery, which matters in Ajman's coastal air, where the finish decides the lifespan.

Why does in-house powder coating matter in Ajman?

Because Ajman is coastal, and coastal air finds every weakness in a cheap finish. Salt-laden humidity attacks thin coating on edges and drill points first. Fabricators who send coating out cannot control preparation or film thickness; we run our own line, so the gate, the fence and the railing for one villa pass through one colour batch with the preparation the coast demands. It is the difference between repainting in two years and not thinking about it for ten.

How does pricing work for Ajman projects?

Architectural pieces are priced after a real site measure, never from a photo, and structural work from your drawings. The quotation is itemised: materials, fabrication, finish, delivery, installation. For a full villa package (gate, fencing, railings, shade) one project manager runs the whole scope on one programme.

Frequently asked questions

Do you serve all of Ajman, including Al Jurf and Masfout?

Yes: villa communities, industrial plots and commercial sites across the emirate, with surveys scheduled within 48 hours.

Can one order cover gate, fencing and railings for a villa?

Yes, and it is the best way to buy them: one measure, one design language, one powder-coating batch, one installation programme, one accountable team.

How long does a fabricated gate take for an Ajman villa?

Typically 2 to 3 weeks from design approval, with railings at 1 to 2 weeks. Committed dates are written into the quotation before fabrication starts.

Is steel suitable for Ajman's coastal climate?

Yes, when prepared and coated properly: the failure mode near the coast is almost always the finish, not the metal. Our in-house powder coating line exists precisely for this.

Do you take small jobs in Ajman?

Yes. A single railing, one replacement gate leaf or a repair is routine work, alongside full villa and contractor packages.
