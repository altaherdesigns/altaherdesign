Why does in house fabrication matter?

Most companies quoting for metalwork, aluminium or glass in the UAE do not make any of it. They coordinate a welding shop, a glazier and a coating line, each with its own queue and its own idea of the colour you asked for.

We work the other way round. Cutting, welding, glazing, joinery, stone and powder coating happen in our own workshops in the Al Hail Industrial Area, and our own crews install. A villa gate, its boundary fence, the stair railing inside and the window frames upstairs pass through the same workshop and the same colour batch.

That is why they read as one job rather than four purchases that nearly match.

What does the coast do to metalwork?

Fujairah sits on the Gulf of Oman, as do Dibba and the east coast towns of Kalba and Khorfakkan, and salt air decides how long exterior metal lasts here. The metal is rarely what fails first. The finish is: thin coating on edges, uncovered drill points and poor preparation are where corrosion starts, and none of it shows on handover day.

That is why we run our own coating line. Preparation and film thickness at the edges decide how long a coat lasts, and they are exactly where a cheap job saves money. We specify cast aluminium where corrosion resistance leads, and steel where span and security lead.
