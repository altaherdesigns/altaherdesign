Why does in house fabrication matter?

Most companies quoting for metalwork, aluminium or glass in the UAE do not make any of it. They coordinate a welding shop, a glazier and a coating line, each with its own queue and its own idea of the colour you asked for.

We work the other way round. Cutting, welding, glazing, joinery, stone and powder coating happen in our own workshops in the Al Hail Industrial Area, and our own crews install. A villa gate, its boundary fence, the stair railing inside and the window frames upstairs pass through the same workshop and the same colour batch.

That is why they read as one job rather than four purchases that nearly match.

What does the coast do to metalwork?

Fujairah sits on the Gulf of Oman, as do Dibba and the east coast towns of Kalba and Khorfakkan, and salt air decides how long exterior metal lasts here. The metal is rarely what fails first. The finish is: thin coating on edges, uncovered drill points and poor preparation are where corrosion starts, and none of it shows on handover day.

That is why we run our own coating line. Preparation and film thickness at the edges decide how long a coat lasts, and they are exactly where a cheap job saves money. We specify cast aluminium where corrosion resistance leads, and steel where span and security lead.

How does a project run?

Four steps, the same for a single gate or a whole villa.

  1. Free site visit within 48 hours

    Anywhere in the seven emirates. We measure what was actually built, not the drawing.

  2. Itemised quotation within 24 hours of the visit

    Materials, fabrication, finish, delivery and installation each on its own line.

  3. Fabrication in our own workshop

    A gate takes 2 to 3 weeks, railings 1 to 2 weeks and a kitchen 3 to 5 weeks.

  4. Installation by our own crew

    Dates are written into the quotation before any work starts.

Questions before you book

Can I get a price from photos before a site visit?

Yes. Send photos and rough sizes on WhatsApp to +971 50 649 9697 and we send a first estimate quickly. The itemised quotation follows the free site visit, because we price what was actually built.

Which towns around Fujairah do you work in?

We work across all seven emirates, and most often close to our workshop: Fujairah City, Dibba, Masafi, Mirbah, Qidfa and Al Bidiyah, the east coast towns of Kalba and Khorfakkan, and Hatta. A site visit in Kalba or Khorfakkan is a short drive from the Al Hail Industrial Area.

Do you make everything yourselves?

Almost everything. We fabricate, powder coat and build the joinery in our own workshop in the Al Hail Industrial Area, and our own team installs it. Laser cutting is done for us by partner workshops; CNC cutting is in house.

Is there a minimum job size?

No. We make a single gate, door or railing as well as a complete villa package, and every job follows the same four steps.

Do you give a warranty?

Yes. Everything we make is guaranteed against manufacturing faults, and the cover depends on the product. Ask for the cover on your item before you approve the quotation.

Can I visit your workshop?

Yes. The workshop in the Al Hail Industrial Area, Fujairah, is open Saturday to Thursday, 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Call or WhatsApp first so the right person is there to show you round.
