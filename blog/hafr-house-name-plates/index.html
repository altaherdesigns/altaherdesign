Introducing Hafr: house name plates and villa signs cut in metal

Al Taher Group has opened Hafr, a new branch for house name plates, house numbers and family names cut in metal, with the Arabic proofed before cutting.

Hafr is the new signage branch of Al Taher Group, making house name plates, house numbers and family names cut in metal for villas in the UAE. Every piece is measured to your wall, set in the script you choose, specified for summer heat, coastal salt and winter sand, and only cut after you approve the Arabic in writing. You can see the full range and start a design at hafr.ae.

Why did Al Taher Group start Hafr?

Because the name on the gate is the one piece every visitor reads. For four decades our work has ended at the gate: the gate itself, the railings and the boundary panels. A family name in Arabic is different work. The letters change shape with their position in the word, the spacing has to read from the street, and a single wrong form is obvious to everyone who passes. That deserves its own team and its own process, so it has its own name: Hafr, حفر, which means carved.

How does Hafr make sure the Arabic name is right?

Nothing reaches the cutting machine until you have approved the name in writing. The process has six steps:

  1. You type the name yourself, in Arabic. It is never transcribed from a voice note.
  2. You choose a setting from two or three treatments of your actual name, not a sample.
  3. You see it on your wall at true proportions with the dimensions marked, on a photo of your own wall where possible.
  4. You sign it off. Your written approval is dated and kept with the job.
  5. A second reader checks it. Another Arabic reader compares the proof with the text you sent.
  6. Cut, finished, photographed. The file is locked, then the piece is cut, finished and photographed before it ships.
"Arabic letters change shape with their position, and one wrong form is obvious to everyone who passes your gate."

Will a metal name plate survive a UAE summer?

Each piece is specified for what it will actually face outside. That means summers of 45 to 50°C, intense sun, coastal salt, seasonal humidity and winter sand, which is the same thinking behind every gate and railing we have built on the east coast since 1984.

Can the name be lit at night?

Yes, from behind or from the front. Lit from behind, the letters float off the wall with a halo. Lit from the front, the name reads clearly from the street. Tell Hafr whether there is power near the wall and the lighting is planned with the piece.

How do I order a house name plate from Hafr?

Start with a photo of your wall. Send these on WhatsApp to +971 50 649 9697:

  • A photo of the gate or wall
  • The rough width available
  • What the wall is made of
  • Which way the wall faces, for sun and shadow
  • Whether there is power nearby, for lighting
  • The name, typed in Arabic

Frequently asked questions

Can a house name plate carry Arabic and English together?

Yes. Hafr sets the family name in Arabic with a Latin transliteration if you want one, and house numbers can use Arabic digits, Latin digits or both.

Who checks the spelling of the Arabic name?

You type the name yourself in Arabic, approve a proof drawn at true proportions in writing, and a second Arabic reader checks the proof against your text before anything is cut.

Does Hafr install the name plate?

Every piece arrives ready to fit, and installation is available on request. Gate and boundary wall panels, screens and plot entrance monuments are always installed by the Hafr team.
