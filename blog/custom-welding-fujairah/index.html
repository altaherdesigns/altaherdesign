Custom welding services in Fujairah, Dibba, Kalba and Khorfakkan

Welding as a full delivery, not just labour on site: measured, fabricated, welded, powder coated and installed by one team from our Fujairah workshop.

Home › Blog › Custom Welding Fujairah

We provide custom welding services from our workshop in the Al Hail Industrial Area, Fujairah, for villas and projects in Fujairah and Dibba, in the east coast towns of Kalba and Khorfakkan, and across the UAE. Since 1984 the family run group has designed, fabricated and installed metalwork for residential and commercial projects in all seven emirates.

For homeowners, villa owners, contractors, developers and commercial property teams, we combine cutting, forming, welding, finishing and powder coating in house with installation by our own team. You work with one welding contractor that takes a job from site measurement to fabrication and final fixing, without passing the scope to outside subcontractors.

What do our custom welding services include?

We handle welding and metal fabrication as a full delivery process, not just labour on site. DAT Metal Fabrication, our fabrication division, works mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium in house, and our own installation team fits the work in Fujairah and Dibba, in the east coast towns of Kalba and Khorfakkan, and across the rest of the UAE.

We have served Fujairah since 1984 and deliver welding and metal fabrication across all seven emirates.

That matters when your project needs more than a welder on site. You may need measured drawings, fabricated sections, finish coordination, powder coating, transport, and installation that fits the wider construction programme. We keep those stages under one roof, which keeps scope, responsibility and handover clear.

Who uses custom welding and fabrication?

We take on single fabrication jobs as well as larger construction packages. Contractors use us for steel frames, mezzanines, brackets, bollards and architectural metalwork. Homeowners use the same workshop for gates, doors, stair railings, balustrades, pergolas, fencing and one off pieces that must fit an existing building accurately.

The practical benefit is flexibility. A contractor can appoint one welding contractor for fabricated steel components, while a homeowner uses the same company for pieces that must suit the finishes, openings and dimensions already on site.

Typical requests include:

  • Structural and utility metalwork: steel frames, custom brackets, mezzanines, sheet metal, bollards and safety barriers.
  • Architectural metalwork: gates, metal doors, stair railings, balustrades, fencing and bespoke pieces.
  • Related fabricated packages: pergolas, shades, car ports, curtain wall supports and facade metal components.

Why do in house welding and powder coating reduce delays?

A welding package usually affects other trades. Openings must match the aluminium or glass, frames must suit cladding or joinery, and finishes must be agreed before installation. We are set up for that, with two specialist divisions and one project manager coordinating across them when the scope includes connected architectural or interior work.

Because we cut, form, weld, finish and powder coat in our own workshops before installation, you avoid the usual split between one fabricator, another finisher and a separate site fixer. That lowers the risk of mismatched dimensions, unclear responsibility and waiting on outside suppliers.

This is especially useful when the welding sits inside a larger package. If your project also needs aluminium, glass, joinery, marble or related fit out work, we coordinate those interfaces through one contractor instead of leaving you to align separate suppliers.

What approvals does welding on site need on UAE projects?

Welding buyers in the UAE should expect more than labour availability. On projects led by a consultant, especially in Dubai, welding on site often needs approval before it starts: the procedure, the certificates and the tests are submitted, and the consultant approves the welding control and quality system first.

That changes how a capable welding contractor plans the job. Our fabrication model moves as much work as possible into a controlled workshop first, so the site scope is defined clearly and unnecessary welding on site is reduced.

For steel structures, agree the inspection and documentation requirements at enquiry stage, so the package is priced and planned for them from the start.

Which metals do we weld?

We work mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium. The choice of metal affects appearance, corrosion resistance, weight, maintenance and cost, so we specify it with you rather than defaulting to one material.

Instead of sourcing each trade separately, you can combine welding and fabrication with related building elements from the same group: shopfront and entrance upgrades, villa boundary works, staircase packages, facade components, partition framing and exterior structures where the metalwork must line up with glass, doors or joinery.

We also run our own powder coating line, so welded items move from fabrication to finishing in the same chain, which keeps colour, surface preparation and scheduling under control.

When are we the right welding contractor for your project?

We are a strong fit when you need a welding contractor that owns fabrication, finish and installation rather than only sending a crew to site. It is especially relevant if your project involves:

  • East coast and UAE delivery: one contractor for Fujairah and Dibba, the east coast towns of Kalba and Khorfakkan, and the other emirates.
  • Custom fabrication: gates, frames, brackets, railings, bollards or structures that need dimensions taken on site and fabrication in the workshop.
  • Joined up coordination: welding that connects to aluminium, glass, joinery, marble or wider fit out work.

Quote speed matters too. A free site visit within 48 hours and an itemised quotation within 24 hours of the visit let you compare scope, quantities and costs early, instead of waiting for a vague estimate.

We are not a marketplace listing or a trading name that outsources the job. We are a family run fabrication and fit out group with a long operating base in Fujairah, our own workshops, our own powder coating line and our own installation team.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer custom welding in Kalba, Khorfakkan and Dibba?

Yes. We fabricate in our Fujairah workshop and install in Dibba and in the east coast towns of Kalba and Khorfakkan, as well as across the rest of the UAE.

Which metals can you weld?

Mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium. We recommend the metal based on exposure, span, appearance and budget, and we can powder coat the finished work on our own line.

Do you weld on site or only in the workshop?

Both, but we move as much welding as possible into the workshop, where quality is easier to control, and keep welding on site to fixing and connections. On projects led by a consultant, welding on site may need approval before it starts.
