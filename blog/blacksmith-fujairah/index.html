Blacksmith in Fujairah: what our metal workshop builds, what it costs and how to choose

Gates, railings, car shades, fencing, structural steel and repairs, cut, welded, powder coated and installed by one team from the Al Hail Industrial Area.

If you are looking for a blacksmith in Fujairah, our metal workshop in the Al Hail Industrial Area builds gates, doors, railings, car shades, sheds, fencing and structural steel, and repairs existing metalwork. It runs as DAT Metal Fabrication, part of Al Taher Group, which has worked from Fujairah since 1984. One team cuts, welds, powder coats and installs, so the job never waits on another shop. Gates start from AED 800 per square metre, railings from AED 700 per running metre, and pergolas and sheds from AED 250 per square metre.

Blacksmith shop or metal fabrication workshop: what is the difference?

The difference is what happens after the welding. A small shop can weld a gate, but the gate usually leaves to be painted somewhere else, or is painted by hand on site. In our workshop the same job passes through every step under one roof:

  1. Measuring on site, so the work is built to the opening as it really is.
  2. Cutting, including CNC cutting on our own machine. Where a design needs laser cut parts, we use specialist partners for that one step.
  3. Forming and welding by our own experienced welders.
  4. Powder coating on our own line, in any RAL colour.
  5. Installation by the same team, with posts set into proper footings.

Powder coating is the step that decides how a gate looks after a few summers near the sea. Our guide to powder coating versus painting explains why.

Steel or aluminium for outdoor metalwork on the east coast?

Steel where strength and span matter, aluminium where the sea is close. Steel is stronger for the same section, so it suits wide gates, long car shade spans and structural work. Aluminium does not rust, so it is the safer choice for plots within reach of salt air in Fujairah, Dibba and the east coast towns of Kalba and Khorfakkan. Both come out of our own powder coating line, so a steel gate and an aluminium railing on the same villa can share one colour batch. For gates in particular, read our guide to custom metal gates in Fujairah.

Can you repair or change existing metalwork?

Yes. Repairs and modifications are part of our work. Typical jobs are strengthening a gate or railing, extending a fence or a shade, and refinishing metalwork that has started to rust. We look at it on site first, because how well a repair lasts depends on what is under the old paint. Where the metal is sound, it can be cleaned back and powder coated to look new.

How do you choose a blacksmith in Fujairah?

Ask five questions before you pay a deposit.

  • Can I visit the workshop where my gate will be made?
  • Will the metal be powder coated, or painted by hand?
  • Who measures the opening, and when?
  • Is the quotation itemised, with metal, finish, hardware and installation on separate lines?
  • Are the fabrication and installation dates written into the quotation?

Our answers are yes, powder coated, our own team within 48 hours, yes, and yes.

"A gate is welded once and lived with for twenty years. We would rather spend an extra hour measuring than have a gate that drags on the ground after its first summer."

Frequently asked questions

How much does a blacksmith charge in Fujairah?

It depends on the job and the metal. As a guide, our gates start from AED 800 per square metre, railings from AED 700 per running metre, and pergolas and sheds from AED 250 per square metre. Every quotation is itemised after a free site visit.

Do you repair existing gates, railings and metalwork?

Yes. Repairs and modifications are part of our work: strengthening, extending and refinishing existing metalwork. We look at it on site first, then quote.

Do you paint the metal or powder coat it?

We powder coat on our own line in the same workshop, in any RAL colour. A baked powder coat holds up to sun and salt air far better than a brushed or sprayed paint.

What are your working hours?

Saturday to Thursday, 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Friday closed.

Do you work in Kalba, Khorfakkan and Dibba?

Yes. Most of our work is on the east coast: Fujairah City, Dibba, Masafi, Mirbah and Qidfa, and the east coast towns of Kalba and Khorfakkan. We also work in Hatta and across all seven emirates.
