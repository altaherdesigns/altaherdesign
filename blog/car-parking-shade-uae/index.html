Car parking shades in the UAE: steel or aluminium, what they cost and what to ask

Steel or aluminium, sheet or slatted roof, and the five things that move the price. Plus the six photos we need to quote your car shade on WhatsApp.

A car parking shade is a metal roof on posts, or on a cantilever frame, that keeps your car out of direct sun, dust and winter rain. In the UAE people also call it a car shade, a car shed, a parking shed or a carport. For most villas we build a powder coated steel frame when the span is wide and an aluminium frame when the house is close to the sea, with a sheet, insulated panel or slatted roof on top. Most single and double villa car shades we quote fall between AED 5,000 and AED 25,000, and take two to three weeks from design approval to handover.

What is a car parking shade, and is a car shed the same thing?

Yes, in everyday UAE use they are the same structure. Homeowners search for a car shade, a car shed, a parking shed or a car park shade, and drawings usually say carport. All of them describe a roof that covers one or more parking bays without being closed on every side. A garage is enclosed and lockable. A car parking shade stays open so heat escapes and the car is easy to reach.

Two layouts cover almost every villa we work on. A post and beam shade stands on two rows of posts and suits wide driveways with room for columns. A cantilever shade carries the roof from posts on one side only, which keeps the driveway clear for opening doors and suits tight side plots where a column line would block access.

Steel or aluminium: which car shade frame lasts on the east coast?

Steel for wide spans and heavy roofs. Aluminium within reach of the sea. Both come out of our own powder coating line, so the choice is about span, weight and salt air rather than looks.

FactorPowder coated steelPowder coated aluminium
Best forThree and four car spans, insulated panel roofsCoastal plots, single and double bays, lighter roofs
RustRusts wherever the coating is chippedDoes not rust
Clear spanLonger for the same section sizeShorter, or needs deeper sections
Weight and footingsHeavier, needs larger footingsLighter, simpler footings
ColourAny RAL colourAny RAL colour
UpkeepYearly check of post bases and fixingsWash down, check fixings

In Fujairah, Dibba and the east coast towns of Kalba and Khorfakkan, salt air finds every chip in a coating. That is why we lean towards aluminium near the sea, and why the steel frames we do build there get extra attention at the welds and post bases before they are coated. Inland, or for a four car span carrying an insulated roof, steel is usually the better engineering answer. Our guide to powder coating versus painting explains why the finish decides how long either metal looks new.

Which roof should a car parking shade have?

If the car sits outside all day, choose an insulated panel. If the look of the driveway matters most, choose slats. The frame is sized to the roof you pick, not the other way round.

RoofUnder the roofWorth knowing
Profiled metal sheetFull shade and rain cover, but the sheet radiates heat unless the roof is high or ventedThe most economical roof
Insulated sandwich panelCoolest underneath, full rain coverHeavier, so the frame is stronger
Slats or fixed louvresFiltered shade, hot air escapes, partial rain coverModern look that matches a pergola
PolycarbonateLets light through and blocks less heat, full rain coverBright, but less cooling

Modern car shade designs often mix the two: an insulated roof over the cars and a slatted side screen facing the street, like the double car shade at the top of this page. Lighting is worth deciding now, so cables run hidden inside the posts and beams instead of being clipped on later.

How much does a car parking shade cost in the UAE?

Most single and double villa car shades we quote land between AED 5,000 and AED 25,000. A price per square metre tells you little on a villa, because five things move the final figure far more than the area does.

Price driverKeeps the price downPushes the price up
Number of cars and spanOne or two baysThree or four bays, long clear spans
FrameStandard steel sections on two rows of postsAluminium, or a cantilever frame
RoofProfiled sheetInsulated panel, slats or louvres
FixingPosts on open groundWall brackets, or new footings cut through existing tiles
ExtrasNoneLighting in the beams, side screens, colour matched to the gate

Because we design, fabricate, powder coat and install with our own team, the quotation is itemised line by line. You see exactly what each choice adds, and there is no reseller margin between you and the workshop that actually builds the shade.

Can a car shade become a lockable metal shed?

Yes. The same frame can be clad on three sides and fitted with doors. A steel shed or aluminium shed beside the car shade is a common way to store tools, bikes, pool equipment or a generator out of the sun. Sheds with doors are where our door workshop comes in: a single door for storage, double doors when a mower or a quad bike has to go in, and glass doors or French doors when the shed doubles as a garden room. Every metal shed we build sits on a raised slab so washdown water and winter rain never pool at the base.

Do you need approval for a car parking shade?

Sometimes. It depends on the emirate, the community and whether the shade fixes to the villa. Fixed outdoor structures can need permission, and gated communities often have their own rules on height, colour and position. We advise during the site visit and prepare the drawings and specifications an application needs, with CAD designs on request.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a car parking shade take to build?

Most villa car shades are fabricated and installed within two to three weeks of design approval. Installation on site is usually one to three days, and the committed dates are written into the quotation.

Will a car shade survive strong shamal winds?

Yes, when it is anchored properly. We set posts into concrete footings sized for wind load rather than surface mounting them, and every roof connection is welded and bolted, not just clamped.

Can you match the car shade to my gate and windows?

Yes. We powder coat on our own line in any RAL colour, so the car shade, the gate and the boundary railings can come out of the same colour batch. Send a photo of the gate and we suggest the closest RAL.

Do you build car parking shades for compounds, shops and staff housing?

Yes. Multi bay layouts for compounds, showrooms and staff accommodation are routine work for us. Contractors and fit out firms can send drawings for a project quotation.
