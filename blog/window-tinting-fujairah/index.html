Window tinting in Fujairah: which film cuts the heat, which gives privacy, and what it costs

Heat rejection, reflective and frosted window films for villas, flats, offices and shops, fitted on your existing glass from AED 45 per square metre.

Window tinting is a thin film fitted to the inside of your existing glass to cut heat, glare and UV, or to add privacy, without replacing the windows. Our glass team in the Al Hail Industrial Area, Fujairah, fits three kinds of film for villas, flats, offices and shops: heat rejection film, reflective mirror film and frosted privacy film. Window tinting starts from AED 45 per square metre, and the warranty depends on the film you choose. We visit free of charge within 48 hours and send an itemised quotation within 24 hours of the visit.

Which window film do you need?

Start from the problem: heat, privacy, or both. Each film solves one of them better than the others.

FilmWhat it doesBest for
Heat rejection (solar) filmCuts heat and UV while keeping the view and the glass looking almost unchangedBedrooms, living rooms and majlis windows that take the afternoon sun
Reflective (mirror) filmStrong heat cut and a mirror look from outside, so people cannot see in during the dayStreet facing windows, offices and shopfront glass in full sun
Frosted (privacy) filmTurns clear glass milky, letting light in but hiding what is behind it day and nightBathrooms, ground floor windows near the street and office partitions

Many homes use two: heat rejection film on the windows that face the sun, and frosted film where privacy matters at night.

Does window tinting really keep a room cooler?

Yes, on the windows that take direct sun. On the east coast, west and south facing glass carries most of the heat into a villa in the afternoon. A heat rejection or reflective film turns part of that heat away at the glass, so the room warms up more slowly and the air conditioning works less. The amount differs from film to film, which is why we quote each film with its supplier data sheet rather than a round number.

Can you put tint film on double glazed or Low E glass?

Often yes, but the film has to suit the glass. A film absorbs some of the sun's heat. On a double glazed unit, or on glass that already has a Low E coating, that heat can build up unevenly across the pane, and in strong sun the glass can crack or the seal of the unit can fail. Before recommending a film we check:

  • whether the window is single or double glazed, and whether it has a Low E coating
  • the size of each pane, because large panes are more sensitive
  • which way the window faces and how much direct sun it takes
  • the film's own rating for use on double glazed glass

Because we make and install double glazed windows ourselves, we know what is inside the frame. Where a film would put the glass at risk, we say so, and where the windows are due for replacement anyway, new Low E double glazing can be the better answer.

What about privacy at night with mirror film?

Reflective film only works while it is brighter outside than inside. During the day it shows a mirror to the street and a clear, slightly darker view from inside. After sunset, with the lights on, the effect reverses and people outside can see in. For bedrooms and bathrooms that need privacy at night, frosted film or curtains do the job that mirror film cannot.

Do you need approval to tint home windows?

The 50 percent limit you may have heard of is for car windows, not homes. For villas and flats, the questions are about appearance: a landlord, a building management or a residential community can have rules on mirror film that changes how the outside of the building looks. If you rent, or live in a managed building or community, check with them before choosing reflective film. Frosted and clear heat rejection films change the outside look far less.

How much does window tinting cost in Fujairah?

Window tinting starts from AED 45 per square metre. The final price depends on:

  • The film: heat rejection, reflective and frosted films are priced differently
  • The glass area: measured pane by pane at the site visit
  • Access: upper floor windows and tall glass need ladders or a platform
  • Old film: peeling or bubbled film has to come off before new film goes on

The warranty depends on the film material and type, and it is written into the quotation with the price.

"The best film for the west window is rarely the best film for the bathroom. We measure each pane, look at where the sun comes from, and quote film by film."

How does a tinting job run?

  1. Free site visit within 48 hours. We measure the glass and check whether it is single or double glazed.
  2. Itemised quotation within 24 hours of the visit, film by film, with the warranty for each.
  3. Fitting by our own glass team. The glass is cleaned and the film is cut to each pane and applied from the inside.
  4. Drying time. Leave the film alone and do not clean the windows for the first days while it dries fully.

Frequently asked questions

Does window tinting really reduce heat in a room?

Yes. A heat rejection or reflective film turns away part of the sun's heat at the glass, so less of it reaches the room and the air conditioning works less. How much depends on the film, so we show you the supplier's data sheet for the film we quote.

Can people see inside at night with mirror film?

Yes. Reflective film gives privacy only while it is brighter outside than inside. After dark, with the lights on, the effect reverses, so rooms that need privacy at night still need curtains or a frosted film.

Can tint film go on double glazed windows?

Often, but not with every film. Film on double glazed or Low E glass can heat the glass unevenly and crack it, so we check the glass type, the pane size and how much sun it takes before recommending a film.

How much does window tinting cost in Fujairah?

Window tinting starts from AED 45 per square metre. The final price depends on the film, the total glass area, how high the windows are and whether old film has to come off first. The quotation is itemised after a free site visit.

Is there a warranty on window film?

Yes. The warranty depends on the film material and type you choose, and it is written into the quotation before work starts.

Do you tint windows in Dibba, Masafi, Kalba and Khorfakkan?

Yes. We work across Fujairah, from Sakamkam, Al Faseel and Merashid to Dibba Al Fujairah, Masafi and Mirbah, and in the east coast towns of Kalba and Khorfakkan, as well as Hatta and all seven emirates.
