Kitchen renovation in the UAE: what to keep, what to replace, and what it costs

New doors and a worktop on sound cabinets, new cabinets on the same layout, or a new layout altogether: how to choose, with honest UAE costs and timelines.

A kitchen renovation does not always mean a new kitchen. If the cabinets are sound and the layout works, new doors, a new worktop and new hardware change the whole room at a fraction of the cost of replacement. If the cabinets are swollen or failing, new cabinets on the same layout is the next step, and moving the sink, the hob or a wall is the biggest job of the three. We build kitchens in our own joinery workshop in the Al Hail Industrial Area, Fujairah, and renovate them across the UAE; this guide is how we help homeowners choose, and what each route costs and takes.

Renovate or replace: which kind of kitchen renovation do you need?

Start with two questions: are the cabinets sound, and does the layout work? The answers put almost every kitchen on one of three routes.

RouteWhat changesWhen it makes sense
RefreshDoors, worktop, handles, hinges and runners; the cabinet boxes stayThe carcasses are dry and solid, and you are happy with where everything is
Replace on the same layoutAll cabinets and the worktop; the sink, hob and power stay where they areThe carcasses are swollen or failing, but the layout works
New layoutCabinets, worktop and the positions of the sink, hob, appliances or wallsThe kitchen does not suit how you cook, or you want to open it to the living room

The first route is the cheapest and the fastest. The last is the one that moves water and power, and that work has to be finished before the new kitchen is made, which is why it takes longest.

How do you know if your cabinets are worth keeping?

Open the cabinet under the sink first. That is where water finds a kitchen's weak point. Signs the carcasses should go:

  • The base panel under the sink is swollen, soft or flaking
  • Edges near the hob or the dishwasher have lifted or bubbled
  • Doors will not stay aligned however often the hinges are adjusted
  • Drawer runners are screwed into board that has started to crumble
  • There are termite trails, or panels that sound hollow

Signs they can stay: dry, solid boxes that are still square, with doors that close properly once the hinges are adjusted. We check the carcasses honestly at the site visit. If they are failing we recommend replacement; if they are sound we quote the refresh, even though it is the smaller job.

What does a kitchen renovation cost in the UAE?

Our kitchens start from AED 1,300 per running metre. Across the UAE market, a budget kitchen typically runs AED 1,500 to 2,500 per linear metre, mid range AED 2,500 to 4,500 and premium AED 5,000 and up, and a complete small kitchen often lands between AED 15,000 and 30,000 before appliances. These are UAE ranges, so they cover Dubai and Abu Dhabi as well as the east coast. The three routes sit at different points on that scale:

  • Refresh: you pay for doors, worktop and hardware, not for new carcasses, which is why it costs a fraction of replacement
  • Replace on the same layout: priced like a new fitted kitchen, plus removing the old one
  • New layout: a new kitchen plus the plumbing, electrical and tiling changes, which are often where renovation budgets grow

Then budget for what the cabinetry quotation does not cover: appliances (easily AED 5,000 to 25,000 and more, depending on brand), the sink and tap (AED 500 to 4,000), a new backsplash, and removing and disposing of the old units. A renovation can cost more per metre than a kitchen in a new villa because of the removal and the work around existing pipes and wiring, but it can also cost less when you keep the layout. How long does a kitchen renovation take?

The workshop time is the same as for a new kitchen: typically 3 to 5 weeks from approved design and final measurements, then 2 to 4 days to install. Lacquered and solid wood finishes add lead time, laminate and acrylic are quicker, and a refresh is quicker again because only the doors and the worktop are made. What changes in a renovation is the order of the work around it:

  1. Plumbing, electrical and tiling first. If any of them change, that work is finished before the final measurement, because a kitchen built to a drawing rather than to the finished room is where fitted kitchens go wrong.
  2. Final measurement, then fabrication in our workshop, so most of the work happens away from your home.
  3. Installation by our own team in 2 to 4 days, depending on size, once the old units are out.
  4. The worktop. Stone worktops are templated on the installed cabinets and fitted after, which adds a short stage but means the stone fits the kitchen as built.

Which upgrades are worth paying for in a renovation?

Spend on the parts you touch every day and the parts water reaches.

  • Hardware: soft close hinges and full extension drawers change how the kitchen feels every day, and cheap runners are usually the first thing to fail.
  • Carcasses near water: BWP plywood or aluminium under the sink and beside the dishwasher, where MDF and particle board suffer first.
  • The worktop: quartz is the most popular UAE choice, hard, non porous and low maintenance; granite is very hard and needs occasional sealing; marble is beautiful but stains and scratches more easily.
  • Deep drawers instead of base cupboards: pans and plates lift out from above instead of being dug out from the back of a cupboard.
  • Lighting under the wall cabinets: it lights the worktop where you actually work.

How does a kitchen renovation run with us?

  1. Free site visit within 48 hours. We map the water, drainage, gas, power and ventilation, check the carcasses and talk through how you cook.
  2. Itemised quotation within 24 hours of the visit, with the cabinets, doors, worktop, hardware and installation each on their own line.
  3. Layout drawing with elevations, and finishes chosen against real samples in your own light.
  4. Built in our Fujairah workshop after the final measurement.
  5. Installed by our own team, worktops templated on the installed cabinets, and a walkthrough at the end: every hinge adjusted, every drawer aligned.

Frequently asked questions

Our kitchens start from AED 1,300 per running metre. Across the market, budget kitchens run around AED 1,500 to 2,500 per linear metre, mid range AED 2,500 to 4,500 and premium AED 5,000 and up, before appliances. A refresh with new doors, worktop and hardware costs a fraction of full replacement.

Can I change only the kitchen doors and the worktop?

Yes, if the carcasses are dry and solid. New doors, a new worktop and new hardware on sound cabinets transform a kitchen at a fraction of the cost of replacement. We check the cabinets at the site visit and tell you honestly which route fits.

How long does a kitchen renovation take?
