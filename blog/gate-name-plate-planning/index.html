A name plate for your gate: what to plan before the gate is made

Where the plate fixes, how the cable reaches it, one colour code for gate and plate, and the order of work, from the team that builds the gates. Plates by Hafr.

The name plate is the last thing fitted at a villa entrance, but the decisions that make it look right belong to the gate stage. As the team that fabricates the gates, we see the same problem often: a fine plate arrives for a pier with no fixing points, no cable and a gate in a slightly different black. This is the checklist we ask homeowners to settle while the gate is still a drawing. The plate itself comes from Hafr, our sister brand for name plates and house numbers cut in metal.

Where should the plate go: pier, wall or gate?

Choose first, because the answer changes the gate drawing.

PositionWhat the gate drawing needsWatch out for
On the pier beside the gateA clear, flat face on the pier at the right height, away from hinges and motorsThe gate motor and the intercom competing for the same spot
On the boundary wallA length of wall with no railing or screen in front of itPlanting that grows across the plate
On the gate leafA flat backing plate welded into the gate frame before coatingExtra weight on the hinges, and clearance as the leaf passes the pier

Hafr's villa name plate size guide explains how big the plate should be for the wall it sits on.

Why should a finished gate never be drilled?

Because every hole breaks the powder coat, and near the sea bare steel at a hole is where rust starts. If the name goes on the gate, we weld a backing plate or fixing points into the frame first, then powder coat the whole gate in one go. Telling us early costs nothing; drilling later costs the finish.

How does the cable reach a lit plate?

Run a conduit to the plate position before the pier is rendered. If the gate has a motor, the electrician is already bringing power to the entrance, so one more conduit to the plate costs little at that stage and a lot after the render. Hafr pieces can be lit from the front, or from behind so the letters float off the wall with a halo. Hafr's guide to backlit and non backlit signs compares the two.

One colour code for the gate, the railings and the plate

Give one RAL code and one gloss level for everything at the entrance. The same RAL code in matte and in gloss looks like two colours in the afternoon sun. Our gates are powder coated on our own line, and Hafr pieces are cut and finished in the Al Taher Group workshop in Fujairah, so both work from the same reference. Hafr cuts 316 stainless steel, powder coated mild steel and aluminium; if the plate is stainless and the gate is coated, choose a gate colour that sits well beside stainless steel.

How is the name checked before cutting?

The plate is the one part of the entrance everyone reads, so the Arabic is approved in writing first. At Hafr you type the name in Arabic yourself, choose from two or three settings of your actual name and see a proof at true proportions. You sign it off in writing, a second Arabic reader checks the proof against your text, and nothing is cut before that approval.

Settle the plate while the gate is still a drawing. Everything after that gets easier: the fixing points, the cable and the colour.

What is the order of work at the entrance?

  1. Choose the plate position and size while the gate is designed.
  2. Add fixing points to the gate drawing if the plate goes on the gate.
  3. Run the conduit to the plate position before the pier is rendered.
  4. Agree one RAL code and gloss level for the gate, railings and plate.
  5. Approve the Hafr proof in writing; the standard turnaround is 7 to 14 days.
  6. Install the gate, finish the pier, then fix the plate last.

Ordering the plate from Hafr

Hafr makes house numbers, family names, crests, gate and boundary wall panels and entrance monuments, from AED 299, with delivery anywhere in the UAE. Start with a photo of your wall at hafr.ae. If the gate, the railings or a car shade are still to come, we design, fabricate, powder coat and install them from our Fujairah workshop.

Frequently asked questions

Should the name plate go on the gate or on the pier?

On the pier or the wall in most cases, because a fixed surface is easier to light and does not move. If there is no pier, the plate can go on the gate, with fixing points welded in before the gate is powder coated.

Can the name plate match the colour of my gate?

Yes. Give one RAL code and one gloss level for the gate and the plate. Our gates are coated on our own line and Hafr pieces are finished in the same group workshop in Fujairah.

When should I order the name plate?

Decide its position and size while the gate is being designed, and order it before the pier is finished, so it is ready to fix last. Hafr's standard turnaround is 7 to 14 days after you approve the proof.

Who makes the name plate?

Hafr, the Al Taher Group line for name plates and house numbers cut in metal. You can start a design at hafr.ae.
