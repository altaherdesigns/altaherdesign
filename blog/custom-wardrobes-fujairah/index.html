Custom wardrobes in Fujairah: hinged, sliding or walk in, and what to decide first

Hinged or sliding doors, a wall of wardrobes or a walk in, a wood or aluminium carcass, and how the inside is planned around what you actually store.

A custom wardrobe is joinery built to your wall, up to the ceiling, with the inside planned around what you actually store. We build them in our own carpentry workshop in the Al Hail Industrial Area, Fujairah, the workshop where Al Taher began in 1984, and fit them with our own team. Most homeowners have three decisions to make: hinged or sliding doors, a wall of wardrobes or a walk in, and a wood or an aluminium carcass for the room it stands in.

Hinged, sliding or walk in: which suits the room?

Let the floor in front of the wardrobe decide. Hinged doors need clear floor to swing open. Sliding doors need none, but only part of the width opens at a time. A walk in closet needs a room or a deep alcove of its own.

Hinged doorsBedrooms with clear floor in front of the wardrobeThe whole width opens at once, and every finish and handle works
Sliding doorsTighter rooms where a door would hit the bed or a bedside tableHalf the width opens at a time; the track and rollers decide how it feels after years
Walk inA spare room, a deep alcove or a dressing area beside the bathroomEverything is in view, so lighting and dust matter more than doors
Open shelvingDressing rooms and children's roomsFewer doors to pay for, but everything on show collects dust

How do you plan the inside of a wardrobe?

Count what goes in before choosing what goes on the front. The inside is where a custom wardrobe earns its keep over a standard one. At the site visit we measure the wall and ask what each person keeps, then lay out the sections to match.

  • Long hanging for kanduras, abayas, dresses and coats, which need the full drop
  • Double hanging for shirts and trousers, with two rails one above the other
  • Drawers for folded clothes, with a shallow one for watches and accessories
  • Shoe shelves near the bottom, where they are easy to reach
  • Top cabinets up to the ceiling for suitcases and winter bedding
  • A mirror inside a door, or a full height mirror panel on the front

Building up to the ceiling also stops dust settling on top of the wardrobe, which on the east coast is a real point in its favour.

Wood or aluminium inside a UAE bedroom?

Wood and board for dry rooms; aluminium where a wall stays damp. Bedrooms are drier than kitchens, so both work. Wardrobes against an outside wall, or a wall shared with a bathroom, do better with aluminium carcasses, because aluminium does not swell, warp or attract termites. A wood look finish on aluminium keeps the warmth of timber on the front.

Doors, finishes and hardware

The fronts are what you see; the hinges and runners are what you feel every day. Doors can be painted, veneered or laminated, made in wood look aluminium, or faced with mirror and glass, which our glass team cuts and edges to size. Hardware decides whether the doors still close softly and the drawers still run smoothly after thousands of openings, so we specify it before we choose a colour.

We measure the room as it was built, not as it was drawn. Walls in a new villa are rarely square, and a wardrobe built to a drawing ends up with gaps filled with trim.

TV units, dressing tables and the rest of the room

The same workshop builds the TV wall, the dressing table and the bedroom doors, so the room can share one finish. TV walls come with panelling, open shelves and closed cabinets where you want them, and the cable and socket positions are agreed at the site visit. Dressing tables are built with the mirror and lighting planned in, and timber and WPC doors are matched to the rest of the joinery.

How does a wardrobe project run?

  1. Free site visit within 48 hours. We measure the walls, floor and ceiling as built and talk through what you store.
  2. Itemised quotation within 24 hours of the visit. Carcass, doors, hardware and installation each on their own line.
  3. Built in our carpentry workshop in the Al Hail Industrial Area.
  4. Installed by our own team, on dates written into the quotation before any work starts.

Frequently asked questions

Are sliding or hinged wardrobe doors better?

Hinged doors open the whole width at once and suit rooms with clear floor in front. Sliding doors suit tighter rooms where a door would hit the bed, at the cost of opening half the width at a time.

Can a wardrobe go all the way to the ceiling?

Yes. We measure the ceiling height at the site visit and build top cabinets to it, which adds storage for suitcases and stops dust settling on top.

Is aluminium or wood better for a wardrobe in the UAE?

Both work in a dry bedroom. Against an outside wall or a wall shared with a bathroom, aluminium is the safer choice because it does not swell or attract termites, and a wood look finish keeps a warm front.

Do you build walk in closets?

Yes. Walk in closets are built with hanging, shelves, drawers and lighting planned for the room, in the same workshop as our wardrobes and kitchens.

Can the wardrobe match the bedroom doors and the TV unit?

Yes. Wardrobes, TV units and timber or WPC doors come from the same carpentry workshop, so they can share one finish and colour.

Do you fit wardrobes in Kalba, Khorfakkan and Hatta?

Yes, in the east coast towns of Kalba and Khorfakkan, in Hatta and across Fujairah, from Dibba and Masafi to Mirbah. The site visit is free and the itemised quotation follows within 24 hours.
