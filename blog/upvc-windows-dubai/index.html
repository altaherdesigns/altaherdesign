uPVC Windows in Dubai
Choosing a Supplier

Heat, humidity and compliance decide the specification. Here is what separates a supplier who fits windows from one who solves the problem.

Home › Blog › uPVC Windows in Dubai: Choosing a Supplier

Choosing uPVC windows in Dubai is a heat, humidity and compliance decision before it is a price decision. Summers here sit around the low forties with overnight lows near thirty and annual humidity around sixty per cent, which means glazing and frames spend months resisting solar gain and condensation risk at once. Al Taher Group supplies and fits uPVC window and door systems from its Fujairah workshop, serving Dubai alongside Kalba, Khorfakkan and all seven emirates.

The short version

  • Specify for heat, humidity and daylight first. Frame colour and profile style come after.
  • Multi-chamber profiles with steel reinforcement matter more in this climate than chamber count alone.
  • Dubai Municipality sets requirements around openable area, daylight and glazing performance. Confirm who handles compliance.
  • We supply and install uPVC, we do not extrude it. Our in-house fabrication is aluminium, steel, glass and joinery.
  • Compare quotes on profile, reinforcement, glazing spec, hardware and installation, not on window count.

What does the Dubai climate demand?

Two things at once: keep heat out, and stop condensation forming where warm humid air meets cooled surfaces. uPVC helps with both because the profile is a poor conductor by nature. Air trapped in the chambers slows heat transfer without needing the thermal break an aluminium frame requires.

The glazing does most of the work regardless of frame material. A double glazed unit with a Low-E coating reflects infrared heat while letting daylight through, and in this climate that is the single most effective upgrade per dirham spent. We specify double glazing rather than triple, because triple adds weight and cost for little return where the problem is heat coming in rather than heat escaping.

What separates a good profile from a cheap one?

Chamber count, wall thickness and steel reinforcement. More chambers generally means better insulation, but a five chamber profile with thin walls and no reinforcement will still sag on a wide opening after a few summers. Ask for the reinforcement detail, not just the chamber number.

Reinforcement matters specifically here because uPVC expands and contracts with temperature more than aluminium does. On large sashes and sliding panels, galvanised steel inside the profile is what stops the frame bowing or the sash dropping out of alignment. This is the failure most homeowners only notice in year three.

Casement, tilt and turn, or sliding?

Casement seals tightest against dust and noise because the sash compresses into the frame, which suits bedrooms and street-facing elevations. Tilt and turn gives secure ventilation in the tilted position and full opening for cleaning, which is why it suits upper floors. Sliding consumes no room space and handles wide openings onto balconies and terraces.

Most Dubai villas end up with a mix rather than one system throughout, and that is correct. The brief should assign systems room by room rather than picking one and applying it everywhere.

Who handles Dubai Municipality compliance?

Requirements around openable area, daylight provision and glazing performance apply to residential work, and the practical question at enquiry stage is simply who is responsible for confirming compliance and preparing anything an approval needs. A supplier who cannot answer that clearly is telling you something useful.

For replacement work in an existing villa this is usually straightforward. For new build or significant alteration it is worth raising before fabrication rather than after, because a window schedule that fails on openable area is an expensive correction.

What should you expect from installation?

Measurement of the actual opening, not the drawing. Openings vary once plaster, tiling and sills are in, and a frame cut to a drawing arrives needing packers and sealant to hide the gap. That gap is where air and water find their way in later.

Expect the crew to check the opening square and level, set the frame plumb with proper fixings into structure, seal and insulate the perimeter correctly, and test that every sash closes and locks before leaving. Replacement in an occupied villa should run room by room, with each opening made weather-tight the same day it is opened.

How do you compare uPVC quotes?

Normalise the scope before comparing totals. Ask each supplier to state: the profile system and chamber count, the reinforcement detail, the glazing specification including Low-E and unit thickness, the hardware brand and type, whether measurement, removal of old frames, installation, sealing and debris removal are included, and the warranty on profile, hardware and installation separately.

A quote listing window count and a total is not cheaper, it is incomplete. And one honest note on us: we supply and install uPVC systems rather than extruding them ourselves. Frequently Asked Questions

Are uPVC windows suitable for Dubai heat?

Yes, when specified properly. The profile is a poor conductor and the chambers trap air, which slows heat transfer without needing a thermal break. What matters alongside it is the glazing, since a double glazed unit with a Low-E coating does most of the work in this climate.

What profile should I ask for?

Ask for chamber count, wall thickness and steel reinforcement together rather than chamber count alone. Reinforcement matters most on wide sashes and sliding panels, because it is what stops the frame bowing or the sash dropping out of alignment after a few summers.

Do uPVC windows need Dubai Municipality approval?

Requirements around openable area, daylight and glazing performance apply to residential work. The practical question is who confirms compliance and prepares anything an approval needs, and it is worth settling that at enquiry stage rather than after fabrication.

Do you manufacture uPVC windows yourselves?

No. We supply and install uPVC window and door systems. Our in-house manufacturing is aluminium, steel, glass and joinery from the Fujairah workshop. We prefer to state that boundary clearly.

uPVC or aluminium for a Dubai villa?

Both, in different rooms. uPVC suits standard openings and balcony doors where insulation and cost lead. Aluminium suits wide openings with slim frames, large sliding panels and folding systems where structural strength leads.

Do you install uPVC windows in Dubai?

Yes. Supply and installation across Dubai and all seven emirates, with the workshop and crews based in Fujairah and regular work across Kalba and Khorfakkan on the east coast.
