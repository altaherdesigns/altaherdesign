How should you care for a marble countertop after installation?

Marble needs careful handling even after a good installation. It is a calcareous stone, so acidic cleaners such as vinegar dull the surface and should be avoided.

Our marble service includes sealing and polishing, and that matters because marble is porous. Sealing helps the countertop resist staining, but it does not make the stone stain proof, so the daily routine still counts.

For everyday cleaning, use a mild pH neutral cleaner. Warm water with a little mild dish soap works well, and coasters, trivets and mats reduce etching, heat marks and abrasion on kitchen and bathroom marble.

Resealing may be needed from time to time, depending on use. A simple check every couple of months is to see whether water still beads on the surface. If it soaks in and darkens the stone, it is time to reseal.

Should a marble countertop be measured before or after the cabinets are fitted?

After. We survey once the base cabinets are fixed and level, so the stone is cut to the real positions rather than to a drawing. Because we also make kitchens and joinery, both can be sequenced under one project manager.

Does sealed marble still need care?

Yes. Sealing helps marble resist stains but does not make it stain proof. Clean with warm water and a mild pH neutral cleaner, avoid acidic products such as vinegar, and reseal when water stops beading on the surface.
