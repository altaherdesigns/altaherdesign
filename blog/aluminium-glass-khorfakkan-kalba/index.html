Aluminium and glass in Khorfakkan and Kalba: what the sea air changes

Windows, doors, shower glass and balustrades for homes in the east coast towns of Kalba and Khorfakkan, and the choices that decide how long they last by the sea.

Yes, we make and install aluminium windows, doors and glass for homes in the east coast towns of Kalba and Khorfakkan, from our workshop in the Al Hail Industrial Area, Fujairah. Both towns sit on the Gulf of Oman with the sea a few streets away, and on that coast the salt in the air decides more about how long a window, a door or a balustrade lasts than the brand on the profile. This guide covers what salt air does, which choices hold up, and how a survey works.

What does salt air do to aluminium and glass?

Aluminium does not rust, but its coating and the fittings around it take the brunt of the salt. Salt settles on every surface with the morning humidity, then dries in the sun, day after day. Where a coating is thin or chipped, the metal underneath starts to pit. On a balcony, the fittings that hold the glass usually corrode first, not the glass and not the frame.

That is why we specify the frame, the coating and the fittings together, and why the aluminium we fabricate is powder coated on our own line rather than sent out.

Aluminium or uPVC for a house near the sea?

Both stand up to salt air, so choose opening by opening. We supply and install both, and on most villas the answer is a mix.

Which glass for showers, partitions and balconies?

Framed glass seals better and costs less; frameless looks cleaner and needs thicker glass. Framed systems hold the glass in aluminium profiles, which gives better acoustic sealing and easier door alignment. Frameless glass does structural work itself, so it is typically 10 to 12 mm toughened rather than 6 to 8 mm.

  • Shower enclosures: toughened safety glass, templated after the tiles are on, so the screen fits the walls as they are
  • Balustrades: toughened or laminated glass in channels, clamps or standoff pins, with fittings chosen for the salt air
  • Balcony enclosures and glass roofs: laminated safety glass in powder coated aluminium frames
  • Mirrors: bathroom, wardrobe and feature mirrors cut and edged to size

Is double glazing worth it on the east coast?

Yes for rooms that face the sun. Double glazing slows the heat coming in through the glass, so rooms stay cooler and the air conditioning works less, and Low E coated glass reflects more of that heat. We fit double glazed units in both aluminium and uPVC frames. Read the benefits of double glazed windows in the UAE.

Near the sea, the fittings decide the life of a glass balustrade more than the glass does, so we choose the glass and its fittings together.

How does a survey work in Khorfakkan and Kalba?

  1. Free site visit within 48 hours. We measure every opening as it was built, not as it was drawn.
  2. Itemised quotation within 24 hours of the visit. Profiles, glass, coating, fittings and installation each on their own line.
  3. Made in our Al Hail workshop. Frames are cut and coated in house; the glass is made to our measurements and fitted by our own team.
  4. Installed by our own team, on dates written into the quotation.

Where else do we work?

From the same workshop we work across Fujairah, in Fujairah City, Dibba, Masafi, Mirbah, Qidfa and Al Bidiyah. We also work in Hatta, and across all seven emirates. From Kalba or Khorfakkan, the workshop is a short drive away.

Frequently asked questions

Do you install aluminium windows in Khorfakkan?

Yes. We measure, make and install aluminium windows and doors for homes in Khorfakkan, with the frames powder coated on our own line and the fittings chosen for the salt air.

Do you do glass work in Kalba?

Yes. Shower enclosures, partitions, balustrades, balcony enclosures and mirrors are templated on site in Kalba, made to our measurements and fitted by our own team.

Which lasts longer near the sea, aluminium or uPVC?

Both resist salt air. Aluminium does not rust and depends on its coating; uPVC does not corrode. We often use both on one villa, chosen opening by opening.

How soon can you visit a house in Kalba or Khorfakkan?

Within 48 hours of your message, free of charge, and the itemised quotation follows within 24 hours of the visit.
