Metal, aluminium and glass works in Sharjah

Gates, railings, aluminium windows, glass works and fit-out delivered into Sharjah from our Fujairah workshop, part of 600+ completed projects across the two emirates since 1984.

Sharjah is the second home of Al Taher's project book. Alongside Fujairah it makes up the bulk of our 600+ completed projects since 1984: villa gates and railings, aluminium and glass packages, fitted kitchens, and commercial fabrication. Everything is made in our own Al Hail workshop and installed by our own team. Free site visit within 48 hours, itemised quote within 24 hours.

How we work

  1. Free site visit within 48 hours. We measure the actual opening or structure, no guessing from photographs.
  2. Itemised quotation within 24 hours of that visit. Materials, finish, hardware and installation priced line by line.
  3. Fabrication in our own Fujairah workshop. Metal, aluminium, glass, joinery and powder coating in-house, no subcontracting.
  4. Installation by our own team, with committed dates written into the quotation.

What do we build for Sharjah properties?

  • Villa gates: cast aluminium and steel, sliding, swing and automated. Design guide
  • Stair railings and balustrades: stainless steel, glass, aluminium and wrought iron.
  • Aluminium and uPVC windows and doors: thermal break profiles and double glazing. Spec guide
  • Glass works: partitions, shower enclosures, balustrades and shopfronts.
  • Steel fabrication: structural steel, sheet metal, fencing and bollards for contractors.
  • Fitted kitchens, joinery, pergolas and marble work.

Does being based in Fujairah affect Sharjah projects?

Honestly: it changes the logistics, not the quality. We are a Fujairah workshop that has worked in Sharjah for decades. The drive is routine for our installation teams and deliveries are planned around it. What you gain in exchange is a fabricator that owns every step: cutting, welding, glazing, powder coating and installation all happen under one roof, so nothing is subcontracted and one team is accountable for the result. For an urgent same-day callout a Sharjah-based workshop will be quicker; for a specified villa or commercial package, in-house control is the stronger trade.

Who do we work with in Sharjah?

Both homeowners and trade. Contractors and interior fit-out firms use us as the metal, aluminium and glass side of their package, the trades most fit-out companies do not fabricate themselves. Frequently asked questions

Do you actually work in Sharjah or only Fujairah?

Both. Sharjah and Fujairah together account for the bulk of our 600+ completed projects since 1984. Our workshop is in Fujairah's Al Hail Industrial Area and our installation teams travel to Sharjah routinely.

Is there an extra charge for working in Sharjah?

Site visits are free and quotations are itemised, so any mobilisation or delivery cost appears as a line item rather than hidden in the price. For most villa-scale projects it is a minor component.

How fast can you get to a Sharjah site?

We commit to a free site visit within 48 hours and send the itemised quotation within 24 hours of that visit.

Do you supply contractors and fit-out firms in Sharjah?

Yes. We work as the metal, aluminium and glass partner for interior fit-out and contracting firms: the trades most fit-out companies do not have in-house. We can quote from drawings and work to a main contractor's programme.

Can you handle metalwork and joinery on one project?

Yes. That is the point of our two divisions. Carpentry, aluminium and metal forming sit alongside DAT Metal Fabrication on the same premises, so mixed packages run under one project manager and one schedule.
