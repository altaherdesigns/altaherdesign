Al Taher Group fabricates and installs metalwork, aluminium, glass and facade systems for Dubai projects from our workshop in the Al Hail Industrial Area, Fujairah. How we work

  1. Free site visit within 48 hours — we measure the actual structure, not a drawing.
  2. Itemised quotation within 24 hours of the visit — priced line by line.
  3. Fabrication in our own Fujairah workshop — no subcontracting.
  4. Installation by our own team, with committed dates in the quotation.

Why would a Dubai project use a Fujairah workshop?

Because of what stays in-house rather than where the workshop sits. Dubai has no shortage of aluminium and glass suppliers, but a large share of them subcontract fabrication, finishing or both — which is where programme slippage and quality inconsistency enter a project. We cut, weld, glaze, powder-coat and install with our own team, so a single party is accountable from drawing to handover. For fit-out firms in particular, metal, aluminium, glass and powder coating are precisely the trades usually missing internally, and buying them as one package removes an interface rather than adding one.

How do deliveries and programme work?

Fujairah to Dubai is a routine run for our installation teams, and it is planned into the schedule rather than improvised. In practice this means fabrication windows are agreed against your programme, deliveries are consolidated rather than piecemeal, and installation crews mobilise for defined periods. Honestly stated: for a same-day emergency callout, a Dubai-based workshop will always be faster. For a specified package with a known programme — which is what most Dubai commercial and villa work is — the in-house control is the better trade.

Which services do we deliver here?

  • Custom metal gates — cast aluminium, steel and automated systems.
  • Stair railings and balustrades — stainless steel, glass, aluminium and iron.
  • Aluminium windows and doors — thermal break profiles and Low-E glazing.
  • Glass works — partitions, shower enclosures and balustrades.
  • Metal and steel fabrication — structural steel, sheet metal, bollards and fencing.
  • Curtain walls and facade systems — stick and unitised glazing.

What do contractors and fit-out firms buy from us?

Typically the metal and glazing scope of a wider package: curtain walling and structural glazing, aluminium cladding and sub-frames, shopfronts and entrance screens, structural steel and sheet metal, balustrades, and powder coating of fabricated elements. Do you work with homeowners in Dubai?

Yes — villa gates, railings, windows, kitchens and pergolas, on the same 48-hour site visit and 24-hour quotation basis. The practical consideration for a homeowner is programme rather than price: if you need work done within days, a local workshop suits better; if you are planning a villa package over weeks, our fabrication control is worth the drive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Al Taher work on projects in Dubai?

Yes. We fabricate and install metalwork, aluminium, glass, curtain walling and facade systems for Dubai projects from our Fujairah workshop, for contractors, fit-out firms, developers and direct clients.

Is there an extra cost for working in Dubai?

Site visits are free and quotations itemised, so any mobilisation or delivery element appears as a visible line rather than being hidden in the rate. On package-scale work it is a small proportion of the total.

How quickly can you attend a Dubai site?

We commit to a free site visit within 48 hours and issue the itemised quotation within 24 hours of that visit.

Do you take subcontract packages from main contractors?

Yes. We quote from tender drawings, work to main contractor programmes and carry out our own site survey, fabrication, finishing and installation.

What is the advantage over a Dubai-based fabricator?

Everything stays in-house — cutting, welding, glazing, powder coating and installation — so one party is accountable throughout. A Dubai workshop wins on emergency response time; we win on control of the finished result.
