Forty years. One standard.

From a single carpentry workshop in Fujairah in 1984 to two divisions serving homeowners and contractors across all seven emirates, Al Taher Group is still family run and still based in the Al Hail Industrial Area.

How did we get here?

  1. 1984

    Founded in Fujairah

    Al Taher Group begins as a small carpentry and joinery workshop in the Al Hail Industrial Area, Fujairah, taking on villa fit out work across the emirate.

  2. 1990s

    Expanding into aluminium and glass

    As Fujairah grew through the nineties, we added aluminium and uPVC windows, doors, glass works and marble, serving developers building the new residential districts.

  3. 2000s

    Metal fabrication becomes a specialism

    Demand for custom gates, railings and architectural metalwork led us to formalise the metal side, adding experienced welders, structural steel work and, in time, our own powder coating line.

  4. 2010s

    Two divisions, one group

    The business was structured as two licensed divisions: Al Taher Carpentry, Aluminium & Metal Forming LLC for joinery, kitchens, aluminium and glass, and DAT Metal Fabrication for gates, railings, steel fabrication and powder coating.

  5. Today

    Two workshops, all seven emirates

    Homeowners, villa developers and contractors across the UAE, served from the same industrial area we started in, with the work still made and installed by our own team.

One project manager across both workshops

When a project needs both sides, say a fitted kitchen with a steel frame or a marble staircase with metal balustrades, you do not coordinate two companies. One project manager owns the job from the first site visit to handover and schedules both workshops around your dates.

The divisions exist for licensing and specialisation. They work from two nearby premises under the same family, and you keep one WhatsApp thread from start to finish.
