Why does Fujairah's coast change the specification?

Salt air is the deciding factor, and it attacks the finish and the fittings before it touches the glass. Cheap or thin coatings blister and chalk within a couple of seasons on the East Coast; low-grade hardware on an outdoor balustrade seizes or corrodes long before the panel fails. So for coastal work we specify properly applied powder coating at adequate film thickness (done on our own line, not outsourced) and corrosion-resistant stainless or powder-coated fittings on anything exposed. Frequently asked questions

Do you supply and install, or supply only?

Both, though the great majority of our work is supply and install. We measure the actual opening, fabricate to those dimensions and fit with our own team, which is where most window and glass problems are avoided.

Can you replace glass in existing aluminium frames?

Yes. We re-glaze existing frames as well as supplying complete new systems, including toughened and laminated replacements for shower enclosures and balustrades.

What glass is used for showers and balustrades?

Safety glass in both cases: toughened for shower enclosures, and toughened or toughened-laminated for balustrades depending on the design and whether a continuous handrail is present. Ordinary annealed glass is not acceptable for either.

Do you fit uPVC as well as aluminium?

Yes, and we treat them as complementary. Aluminium carries large glass with slim frames; uPVC insulates better on smaller openings. Most villas end up with a mix, specified opening by opening.
