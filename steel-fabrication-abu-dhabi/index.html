Steel Fabrication
in Abu Dhabi

Structural steel, gates, railings and architectural metalwork for Abu Dhabi — fabricated in our own workshop, coated on our own line, installed by our own crew.

Home › Areas › Steel Fabrication Abu Dhabi

Abu Dhabi projects comparing fabrication companies are usually weighing two models: a coordinator who subcontracts the making, or a fabricator who owns the workshop. Al Taher Group is the second kind — fabricating structural steel, sheet metal, gates, railings and architectural metalwork in our own facility since 1984, finishing on our own powder-coating line, and installing across Abu Dhabi with our own crew. Surveys are scheduled precisely; quotations are itemised within 24 hours of the visit.

What fabrication work do we deliver in Abu Dhabi?

The emirate's scale means two distinct streams. Commercial and contractor packages: structural steel from tender drawings, mezzanines, canopies, balustrade packages for buildings, and safety bollards for forecourts and loading areas. Villa work in Khalifa City, MBZ City, Al Reef, Al Shamkha and the island communities: custom gates, stair railings, pergolas and car shades, and cladding elements. Compliance documentation — material certificates, drawings for approval — is prepared as part of the package, not as an afterthought.

How do we run Abu Dhabi projects from Fujairah?

Distance changes the logistics, not the fabrication. We batch Abu Dhabi work into planned survey and installation windows, confirm dimensions before a single section is cut, and deliver finished, coated work ready to fix. The quotation shows any transport as its own line. What the distance buys you is a fully integrated workshop: cutting, welding, finishing and coating under one roof, with no third-party queue deciding your programme.

Do we work with Abu Dhabi contractors and consultants?

Yes — pricing from tender drawings, shop drawings for approval where the design warrants them, our own site surveys, and installation to the main contractor's programme. For estidama-conscious projects, our aluminium and glazing scope is specified with thermal performance in mind — see aluminium windows and doors and curtain walling.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Abu Dhabi areas do you cover?

The emirate broadly — Abu Dhabi city and island communities, Khalifa City, MBZ City, Al Reef, Al Shamkha, Mussafah's industrial zone and Al Ain by arrangement.

Can you price a package from tender drawings?

Yes — send the drawings and we return an itemised quotation against them, then confirm critical dimensions on site before fabrication begins.

Do you provide material certificates and approval drawings?

Yes — mill certificates, finish specifications and shop drawings for approval are part of contractor packages, prepared before fabrication rather than assembled afterwards.

What lead times should an Abu Dhabi project expect?

Fabrication times match any other emirate — gates typically 2–3 weeks from approval, railing packages 1–2 weeks — with delivery and installation batched into agreed windows.

Do you handle villa work in Abu Dhabi or only commercial?

Both — villa gates, railings and shade structures are a steady share of our Abu Dhabi work alongside contractor packages, priced after a scheduled site measure.
