Metal, aluminium and glass works in Ajman

Villa gates, railings, windows, glass, kitchens and fabrication delivered into Ajman: everything made in our own workshop, installed by our own crews.

Home › Areas We Serve › Ajman

Al Taher Group fabricates and installs metalwork, aluminium, glass and joinery for Ajman from our workshop in the Al Hail Industrial Area, Fujairah. Villa gates, stair railings, windows and glazing, fitted kitchens, pergolas and steel fabrication: cut, welded, powder-coated and fitted by our own team. Free site visit within 48 hours; itemised quotation within 24 hours of the visit.

How we work

  1. Free site visit within 48 hours: we measure the actual structure, not a drawing.
  2. Itemised quotation within 24 hours of the visit: priced line by line.
  3. Fabrication in our own Fujairah workshop: no subcontracting.
  4. Installation by our own team, with committed dates in the quotation.

What does Ajman typically ask us for?

Residential work dominates: villa entrance gates with matching boundary railings, internal staircases and balustrades, window and door packages, and fitted kitchens. Alongside that we supply contractors and fit-out firms with the metal, glass and powder coating scope they do not fabricate internally. Because both sides run out of the same building, a villa package can combine gate, railings, kitchen and glass under one project manager and one schedule.

Which services do we deliver here?

  • Custom metal gates: cast aluminium, steel and automated systems.
  • Stair railings and balustrades: stainless steel, glass, aluminium and iron.
  • Aluminium windows and doors: thermal break profiles and Low-E glazing.
  • Glass works: partitions, shower enclosures and balustrades.
  • Metal and steel fabrication: structural steel, sheet metal, bollards and fencing.
  • Curtain walls and facade systems: stick and unitised glazing.

How do lead times work for Ajman?

The drive changes mobilisation, not fabrication time. Typical lead times hold: a custom gate around 2 to 3 weeks from design approval, railings 1 to 2 weeks, a fitted kitchen 3 to 5 weeks. What we plan carefully is the site visits: measurement up front so fabrication is right first time, then installation in a defined window rather than repeated short trips. Committed dates go into the quotation before any work starts.

Is a Fujairah workshop the right choice for an Ajman villa?

Honestly, it depends on the job. For a defined package (a gate with matching railings, a window schedule, a kitchen), the in-house control is worth it: nothing is subcontracted, finishes match because they run through one powder coating batch, and one team is accountable. For a small urgent repair needing someone on site the same afternoon, a workshop inside Ajman will serve you better, and we would rather tell you that than take work we cannot service properly.

Frequently asked questions

Does Al Taher deliver and install in Ajman?

Yes. Fabrication happens in our Fujairah workshop and installation is carried out by our own team across Ajman: gates, railings, windows, glass, kitchens, pergolas and fabrication work.

What are typical lead times for Ajman projects?

Once design is approved: gates roughly 2 to 3 weeks, stair railings 1 to 2 weeks, fitted kitchens 3 to 5 weeks. Distance affects visit scheduling rather than fabrication time, and committed dates go into the quotation.

Is there an extra charge for delivery to Ajman?

Any mobilisation or delivery element appears as an itemised line in the quotation rather than being hidden in the rate, so you can see exactly what it contributes.

Do you supply contractors in Ajman as well as homeowners?

Yes. Fit-out and contracting firms use us for the metal, aluminium, glass and powder coating scope they do not hold in-house, quoted from drawings and worked to their programme.

Can one project cover gates, kitchen and glass together?

Yes. That is what our two divisions on one premises are for. Mixed packages run under a single project manager with one quotation and one schedule.
